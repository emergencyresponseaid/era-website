Who We Are

Community-led support
for a barrier-free society

ERA is a registered Community Based Organization (CBO) under the Social Development Office in Dadaab, Kenya — committed to transforming the lives of persons with disabilities.

Vision
A barrier-free society where persons with disabilities enjoy dignity, equality, and full participation.
Mission
To advance inclusion, accessibility and rehabilitation for persons with disabilities through advocacy, capacity building and community support.

Our Objectives

What we stand for

Three pillars guide everything ERA does — services, protection, and inclusion.

♿
Accessible Services
Provide accessible services, rehabilitation support and assistive devices to persons with disabilities in Dadaab.
🛡️
Protection & Rights
Strengthen protection, rights and leadership for persons with disabilities within the refugee and host communities.
🌍
Social Inclusion
Promote social inclusion and active participation of persons with disabilities in all aspects of community life.
Core Programs

What we do on the ground

Four key activity areas define ERA's work in the Dadaab Refugee Complex.

01
Rehabilitation
Providing rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities to restore function, improve mobility, and enhance quality of life.
02
Identification & Referrals
Systematic identification, assessment and referral of persons with disabilities to appropriate services and support systems.
03
Awareness Campaigns
Community-wide campaigns to reduce stigma and discrimination against persons with disabilities across Dadaab camps.
04
Capacity Building & Sport
Training programs and inclusive sports activities that build skills, confidence, and social connections among beneficiaries.

Current Challenges

Where we need support

ERA operates mainly through volunteer support and member contributions. Partnerships and funding are urgently needed to expand our reach.

01
Limited funding and resources
Operating primarily on volunteer time and small member contributions limits program scale.
02
Lack of operational support
Administrative and logistical support is needed to sustain and grow operations.
03
Limited training opportunities
Members need ongoing capacity building to improve service quality and impact.
04
Need for stronger partnerships
Broader networks with NGOs, government bodies, and international organizations would amplify ERA's impact.
